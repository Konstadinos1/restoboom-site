The platform

Everything that answers, in one inbox.

Four modules, one price per location. Everything included everywhere.

01Front desk

A front desk that never closes.

SMS, email, Messenger, Instagram, web chat: every conversation in one inbox per location. Missed call? A text goes back in 15 seconds, in the guest's language. The automated front desk answers hours, address and menu questions, day and night — and says it's automated, first thing.

  • Missed-call text in 15 s
  • 5 channels, one inbox
  • “STOP” respected to the letter

02Reviews

Every review deserves an answer.

After the order, RestoBoom asks for the review. Every guest gets the Google link, whatever the rating. A tough review? The manager is alerted. A reply to post? The draft is written, you approve.

  • Review request after every order
  • Suggested replies, you approve
  • No review gating

03Follow-ups

Follow-ups send themselves. On time.

Team dinner, birthday, a guest gone 60 days: sequences that go out within permitted hours and stop dead the moment a guest texts “STOP”.

  • Quiet hours 9 pm to 9 am
  • Instant unsubscribe
  • Consent checked before every send

04Regulars

Know your regulars like a neighbourhood server.

Lifetime spend, last visit, tags: every guest has a profile, fed by your POS data. And smart lists that find the VIPs gone for 30 days in one search.

  • CSV import from day one
  • POS-fed profiles
  • Smart lists
